PeriCam Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026
Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how ep3.works ("we"), the developer of PeriCam (the "app"), handles your information.

1. Overview

The app connects two phones (or a phone and a web browser) directly so that one phone can control the camera app on the other.
We do not collect your personal information, video, photos, or screen contents (except Google Play purchase records and what you send through "Report a bug"; see sections 5 and 6). The app sends no data for advertising or analytics.

3. Information sent to the connected device

Only after you connect two devices and allow screen sharing, the app sends the following directly to the connected device. It never passes through our servers or any third-party server.

  • Video of the host (camera-side) screen (the entire screen while screen sharing is allowed. When the "Allowed apps" restriction is on and Accessibility is enabled, transmission stops while an app that is not allowed is in the foreground, except for the home screen, system screens such as the notification shade, and PeriCam. When the restriction or Accessibility is off, all screens are sent)
  • Still photos added to the camera folder (DCIM/Camera and its subfolders) after starting to wait as the host (sent as the original files; photos taken before connecting are sent after connecting; videos are excluded)
  • Control input and status (from the viewer to the host: taps, pinches, shutter presses, and other controls; from the host to the viewer: status such as shutter results, countdown, paused video, and whether control is allowed. When viewing in a browser, this also includes battery level, temperature, elapsed time, and photo count)

To find a device to connect to, while waiting or searching, the app broadcasts the device name and, if connected to Wi-Fi, its IP address over Bluetooth before screen sharing is allowed.

Connection setup uses Bluetooth Low Energy. Video and photos travel over Wi-Fi Direct, Wi-Fi Aware (on supported devices), a hotspot created by the host phone, or the Wi-Fi network both devices are connected to. Nothing goes over the internet.

When you view in a browser, the host phone serves the viewing page. Access to video, controls, and photos requires a key contained in the QR code. The key and display settings may be stored in the browser.

Between two copies of the app, connections are authenticated with a key exchanged during pairing; unpaired devices cannot connect.

Among photos received on the viewer, only photos for which you tap "Save" are saved in the "Pictures/PeriCam" folder of that device. In a browser, only photos you save are stored in the browser's download location.

5. Purchases and ratings

Paid features are purchased through Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details (such as card numbers); we never receive them. From Google Play we receive only order records (order number, product, price, date and time, country).

The app stores whether you have purchased, when your free trial started, when you first took a photo, whether prompts have been shown, and when the app was last used on the device, and includes this in Android Auto Backup (your Google account backup and device-to-device transfer) so that it carries over when you reinstall or switch devices.

The rating screen shown in the app is a Google Play feature. Your rating is sent to Google Play; we do not receive it through the app.

See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles information.

6. Bug reports

The app sends the following information only when you write a message in "Report a bug" in the app's settings and tap "Send". Nothing is sent automatically.

  • What you entered, app version, device model, Android version, device language setting, and whether the device is a host or viewer
  • Activity records (state history, connection and activity logs, exit reasons recorded by the OS, crash records, and wireless status). Sent only if you select "Attach activity log". IP addresses, MAC addresses, and Wi-Fi network names are redacted before sending. Video, photos, and screen images are not included. App names may be included

You can inspect the records before sending by tapping "View details".

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) to an endpoint we operate on Cloudflare Workers, which stores the submission in our private GitHub repository. The endpoint uses your source IP address only for rate limiting and does not store it.

The submitted information is used to investigate and fix bugs, kept until the investigation and fix are complete, and then deleted.

7. Sharing with third parties

We use the services of Cloudflare, Inc. and GitHub, Inc. to receive and store "Report a bug" submissions. Other than that, we do not share your information with third parties.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

9. Deleting information

You can delete what the app stores on your device from Settings → Apps → PeriCam → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. However, purchase and free trial records included in Auto Backup are not deleted this way; they are removed when you delete your Google account backup. Photos saved on the viewer can be deleted like any other photo. To request deletion of information sent through "Report a bug", contact our email address with the reference number shown after sending.
