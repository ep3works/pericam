PeriCam Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026
Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how ep3.works ("we"), the developer of PeriCam (the "app"), handles your information.

1. Overview

The app connects two phones (or a phone and a web browser) directly so that one phone can control the camera app on the other.
We do not collect your personal information, video, photos, or screen contents (except Google Play purchase records; see section 5). The app has no servers of ours, and it sends no data for advertising or analytics.

3. Information sent to the connected device

Only after you connect two devices and allow screen sharing, the app sends the following directly to the connected device. It never passes through our servers or any third-party server.

  • Video of the host (camera-side) screen, while screen sharing is allowed. Screens of apps not allowed under "Allowed apps" are not sent
  • Photos taken during the session (new photos saved in the host's camera folder)
  • Your control input (taps, pinches, shutter presses), from the viewer to the host

Connection setup uses Bluetooth Low Energy. Video and photos travel over Wi-Fi Direct, a local-only hotspot created by the phone, or the Wi-Fi network both devices are connected to. Nothing goes over the internet.

When you view in a browser, the host phone serves the viewing page itself and the browser opens only that page. Opening it requires a key contained in the QR code.

Between two copies of the app, connections are authenticated with a key exchanged during pairing; unpaired devices cannot connect.

Photos received on the viewer are saved in the "Pictures/PeriCam" folder of that device.

5. Purchases and ratings

Paid features are purchased through Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details (such as card numbers); we never receive them. From Google Play we receive only order records (order number, product, price, date and time, country).

The app stores whether you have purchased and when your free trial started on the device, and includes this in Android Auto Backup (your Google account backup) so that it carries over when you reinstall.

The rating screen shown in the app is a Google Play feature. Your rating is sent to Google Play; we do not receive it through the app.

See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles information.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

8. Deleting information

You can delete what the app stores on your device from Settings → Apps → PeriCam → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Photos saved on the viewer can be deleted like any other photo.

9. Changes

We will announce any changes to this policy on this page. For significant changes, we will also notify you in the app.
